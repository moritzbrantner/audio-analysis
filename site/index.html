measurement guide

What these terms mean

RMS level
Average signal energy; useful for comparing perceived level over time.
Crest factor
Difference between peak and RMS level; larger values mean more transient headroom.
Spectral centroid
The frequency-weighted center of the spectrum; often perceived as brightness.
Stereo correlation
Similarity between left and right channels, from −1 to +1.
Confidence
Estimator-specific certainty, not a universal probability of correctness.
Tempo candidate
An alternative plausible pulse rate, often related by half-time or double-time.
