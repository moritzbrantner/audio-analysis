listen and inspect

DJ overview and musical timeline

Click the timeline or use arrow keys to inspect detected beats.

The overview uses warmer tones for low-frequency energy, green/cyan for mids, and blue/violet for highs; brightness follows local energy. Thin markers are detected beats, taller markers are inferred downbeats, and dashed boundaries are Rust-detected sections. BPM, dominant key, and their confidence are shown in the overview header. The colorized waveform is presentation-only: rhythm, key, and structure remain Rust-owned. Click to seek; Left/Right moves between beats, Shift+Left/Right moves between section boundaries, and Home/End jumps to the track edges.
